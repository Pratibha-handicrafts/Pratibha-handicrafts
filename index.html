Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I send my wedding Varmala flowers for Resin Preservation?
You can drop off your Varmala directly at our shop near Vijay Chowk, Pipraich, Gorakhpur, or send it via local courier. We scientifically dry the petals in silica gel before casting them in crystal-clear epoxy resin.
2. How many days does a Custom Name Bridal Chunri or Ring Platter take?
Custom Name Chunris and Engagement Ring Platters are typically ready within 2 to 4 days. Urgent same-day or next-day express preparation is also available on request!
3. How do I pay for my order? Is Cash on Pick-up available?
Yes! When you place an order here, your complete itemized bill is sent to our official WhatsApp and saved in your 'My Orders' tab. You can pay via UPI (GPay/PhonePe) or Cash on Store Pickup at Pipraich.
